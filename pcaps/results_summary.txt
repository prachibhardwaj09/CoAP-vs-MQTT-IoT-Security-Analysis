KEY FINDINGS:
1. Data Exposure: In plaintext MQTT (TCP port 1883) and CoAP (UDP port 5683), credentials, topics, and JSON payloads are transmitted in cleartext.
2. Transport Overhead: CoAP (UDP) requires only 2 datagrams with 8-byte UDP headers. MQTT (TCP) requires a 3-way handshake and 20-byte TCP headers per segment.
3. Cryptographic Overhead: Applying TLS/DTLS increases packet count and byte volume significantly due to X.509 certificate exchange and record authentication tags.
4. Trade-off: CoAP+DTLS maintains lower bandwidth than MQTT+TLS while achieving complete end-to-end confidentiality.
